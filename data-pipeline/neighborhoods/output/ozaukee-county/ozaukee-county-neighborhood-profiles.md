# Ozaukee County Neighborhood Profiles

**Southeastern Wisconsin Real Estate Relocation Guide**
*Last Updated: February 2026*

---

Ozaukee County is one of southeastern Wisconsin's most desirable residential corridors, stretching along the western shore of Lake Michigan just north of Milwaukee. Known for top-rated school districts, exceptionally low crime rates, and a blend of historic charm and modern suburban living, the county consistently ranks among the safest and most affluent in the state. Whether you are drawn to lakefront living, walkable historic downtowns, or spacious rural lots, Ozaukee County offers a distinct character in each of its seven primary municipalities.

**Commute to downtown Milwaukee:** Approximately 25-30 minutes via I-43 (16 miles) under normal traffic conditions [9].

**Community Vibe:** Mequon blends rural spaciousness with upscale suburban amenities. The local dining and shopping scene has expanded significantly in recent years, centered around the Mequon Town Center.

**Notable Annual Events:**

**Proximity to Lake Michigan:** Direct access -- Mequon's eastern border runs along Lake Michigan, with Virmond Park and Schlitz Audubon offering lakefront access.

**Community Vibe:** Cedarburg is one of the most picturesque small towns in the Midwest. Walking along Washington Avenue feels like stepping back 150 years, with beautifully preserved limestone and brick buildings housing independent boutiques, galleries, and restaurants. Despite its historic charm, the community is far from sleepy -- a packed calendar of festivals and events keeps the downtown buzzing year-round.

**Notable Annual Events:**

**Major Employers:** Mercury Marine (nearby), Cedarburg School District, local small businesses and retail along Washington Avenue [20].

**Community Vibe:** Port Washington is the county seat and heart of Ozaukee County's lakefront identity. The harbor and downtown create a genuine coastal-town atmosphere that is rare in the Midwest. With a thriving restaurant scene, year-round events, and Lake Michigan as the backdrop for daily life, Port Washington has the feel of a New England harbor town transplanted to Wisconsin.

**Notable Annual Events:**
- **Port Washington Pirate Festival** (first weekend in June) -- Approximately 25,000 attendees enjoy pirate-themed entertainment, parades, fireworks, a Thieves Marketplace, and a Buccaneer Bash [39]

**Proximity to Lake Michigan:** Direct lakefront -- Port Washington sits directly on Lake Michigan with a full marina, public beaches, a lighthouse pier, and harbor-front parks.

**Commute to Downtown:** Approximately 35-45 minutes via I-43 (30 miles north of Milwaukee).

**Crime/Safety:** Very safe. Niche reports 'virtually no crime' in the community. Ozaukee County overall has consistently low crime rates. One of the safest communities in southeastern Wisconsin.

### Amenities & Character

**Major Parks, Restaurants & Attractions:**
- **Stony Hill School / National Flag Day Foundation Americanism Center** -- 13,000 sq ft museum with patriotic memorabilia in Waubeka

**Community Vibe:** Tight-knit, family-friendly small town sporting an industrial park, fine school system, new home developments, and the rural charm of yesteryear. Luxembourger immigrant heritage is deeply woven into the community's identity. Neighbors know one another and festivals bring everyone together.

**Notable Annual Events:**
- **Flag Day Celebration** (June) -- Held in Waubeka honoring Fredonia's status as the birthplace of Flag Day (Bernard J. Cigrand held the first observance at Stony Hill School in 1885). Includes parade, ceremonies, and community activities
- **Festival Italia** -- Celebrates Italian heritage with food, music, and activities
- **Miracle on Main Street** (late November) -- Holiday festival bringing the community together
- **Luxembourg Fest** (August, nearby Belgium, WI) -- Celebrates Luxembourg-American heritage with food, music, dancing, and Treipen-eating contest

**Proximity to Lake Michigan / Major Employers:** Approximately 8-10 miles west of Lake Michigan. Not directly lakefront but within easy day-trip distance.. Guy & O'Neill Inc.
