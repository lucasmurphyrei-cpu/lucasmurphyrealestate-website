## County Overview

Waukesha County sits immediately west of Milwaukee County in southeastern Wisconsin and is one of the most desirable residential corridors in the state. With a countywide population exceeding 410,000, the county blends suburban convenience with access to lakes, trails, and highly rated school districts. Waukesha County earns an "A" safety grade from CrimeGrade, ranking in the 89th percentile nationally for overall safety and the 93rd percentile for low violent crime [1]. Major employers in the county include Generac, GE HealthCare, Kohl's (headquartered in Menomonee Falls), Johnson Controls, and Rockwell Automation [2]. While the county does not directly border Lake Michigan, residents are approximately 20-35 miles from Milwaukee's lakefront, making day trips and commutes to the shoreline convenient.

The county's real estate market remains a seller's market as of late 2025, with a countywide median sale price of $495,000 (up 11.5% year-over-year) and average days on market around 44-49 days [3].

### Buyer & Lifestyle Fit

**Ideal Buyer:** Growing families who want Hamilton or Arrowhead schools, a newer house, and land -- the combination that makes Lisbon the most-searched address in northeastern Waukesha County. **Commute to Downtown:** Roughly 25 miles; 30-35 minutes to downtown Milwaukee via WI-74 to US-45 or via I-41. Menomonee Falls is 10-15 minutes east, Brookfield 20 minutes south, and Pewaukee 15 minutes south.

**Walkability & Transportation:** Car-dependent for errands, but far better than a typical rural town for recreation. The Bugline Recreation Trail -- a 16-mile asphalt rail-trail built in 1983 on the old Chicago, Milwaukee, St. Paul & Pacific right-of-way -- runs from Menomonee Falls west through Lisbon to Merton, and is the spine of local walking, running, and cycling. Sussex, entirely surrounded by the village, provides the nearest sidewalks, groceries, and retail.

**Crime/Safety:** Excellent. Lisbon benefits from Waukesha County's low crime profile, with rates below state and national averages. High owner-occupancy and a family-heavy demographic reinforce a safe, quiet character.

- **Fox Brook Park and Menomonee Park** -- Waukesha County parks with swimming beaches within a short drive

**Community Vibe:** Lisbon is the newest village in Wisconsin's oldest suburban county -- it incorporated out of the Town of Lisbon in 2024 after generations of failed attempts, a change driven by residents who wanted control over their own low-density character. The feel is family-first suburban without the density -- new houses, big yards, and a park committee that puts on free community events.

**Notable Annual Events:**

**Proximity to Lake Michigan / Major Employers:** About 35-40 minutes to the Milwaukee lakefront. **Community Vibe:** Summit incorporated as a village in 2010 to control its own future, and the result is a community that has kept lake-country character while quietly becoming a regional service hub.
