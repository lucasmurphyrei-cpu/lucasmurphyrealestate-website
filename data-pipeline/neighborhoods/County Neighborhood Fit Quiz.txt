Title:
Milwaukee County Neighborhood Fit Quiz — Question Logic, Scoring, and Output Mapping

⸻

Prompt:

Act as a real estate data analyst and quiz logic designer.
Use the Milwaukee County neighborhood research report you previously created as the foundation.
Build a complete, working interactive “Neighborhood Fit Quiz” that helps relocation buyers identify which area best fits their lifestyle, budget, and home preferences.

⸻

1️⃣ Objective

Transform the neighborhood data into a structured quiz engine with:
	•	Lifestyle-based questions
	•	Weighted scoring logic
	•	Area-attribute matching
	•	Result copy and tags for CRM/lead capture

The quiz will be embedded on a real estate website (KVCore or Typeform) and needs to output the top 3 neighborhood matches.

⸻

2️⃣ Neighborhoods to Include

Milwaukee County municipalities and key sub-areas:
Milwaukee (city), Bay View, East Side, Wauwatosa, Shorewood, Whitefish Bay, Glendale, Fox Point, River Hills, Brown Deer, West Allis, Greenfield, Greendale, Franklin, Oak Creek, South Milwaukee, St. Francis.

⸻

3️⃣ Deliverables

A. Question Bank (10–14 Questions)
Create multiple-choice questions covering:
	•	Budget
	•	Home type (SFH/condo/townhome)
	•	Beds/baths
	•	Schools importance
	•	Commute time preference
	•	Lifestyle vibe (urban/suburban/luxury/budget)
	•	Historic vs. new homes
	•	Proximity to lake
	•	Nightlife & dining
	•	Yard/garage priority
	•	HOA/condo comfort
	•	Timeline to move
	•	Must-have amenities

For each question:
	•	Include id, question_text, and 3–4 choices (A/B/C/D).
	•	Assign each choice attribute boosts (e.g., A = walkability +5, lake access +3).
	•	Add a weight (1–5) for importance; Budget, Schools, and Commute should be highest (5).

⸻

B. Attribute Model
Define and use these attributes for scoring (0–5 per area):
budget_fit, home_type_fit, school_strength, walkability, nightlife, yard_space, historic_charm, new_construction, commute_downtown, lake_access, safety_reputation, price_psf, tax_sensitivity, hoa_condo_friendly, family_friendly, investor_friendly.

For each area:
	•	Provide 0–5 scores for each attribute with brief justification.
	•	Include median sale price, home style, school summary, commute time, and key lifestyle tags (e.g. #FamilyFriendly #LakeLife #Walkable).

⸻

C. Scoring Logic
	•	Translate each answer into attribute boosts.
	•	Example:
	•	Q: “How important are top-rated schools?”
	•	A: “Very important” → school_strength +5, family_friendly +3.
	•	Total Score = Σ(attribute_score × weighted_answer_boost).
	•	Normalize so the top area’s score ≈ 100.

⸻

D. Tie-Breakers and Guardrails
	•	If top two areas are within 3 points:
	1.	Prioritize budget fit, then school_strength, then commute_downtown.
	•	If budget < median of area by >20% → downrank 8 pts.
	•	If “condo/low-maintenance” selected → uprank condo-friendly areas +6 pts.
	•	If “must have lake proximity” → exclude inland-only areas unless no match.

⸻

E. Output Formats
	1.	JSON (for KVCore/Typeform)
Include:
	•	"questions" with weights and attribute boosts.
	•	"areas" with attributes, tags, and median price.
	•	"scoring" with rules, tie-break logic, and exclusions.
	2.	Markdown Summary (for Website/Notion/Gamma)
	•	Show top 3 matches with badges and short blurbs.
	•	Table format: Area | Why It Fits | Median Price | Commute | Vibe.
	•	CTA placeholders like: “Schedule a Relocation Fit Call.”

⸻

F. Results Copy Templates (Per Area)
For each area, write:
	•	Short version (80–120 words) and Extended version (200–250 words).
	•	Tone: friendly, professional, and relocation-focused.
	•	Include: lifestyle fit, price range, schools, commute, and a friendly CTA.
	•	Example:
“If you love walkable neighborhoods and lake views, Shorewood might be your perfect fit. Expect charming homes, strong schools, and a quick commute downtown.”
	•	Add a cross-suggestion: “Love Shorewood? You may also like Whitefish Bay.”

⸻

G. Lead Capture + CRM Tagging
Provide KVCore-ready tags:
Relocation|MilwaukeeCounty|TopMatch_[Neighborhood]|BudgetRange|LifestyleTag.

Write 5 example email subject lines and a short follow-up email (150–200 words) inviting the user to schedule a Relocation Fit Call.

⸻

H. Validation Examples
Show 3 sample personas with:
	•	Answers → Attribute Boosts → Final Top 3 Results.
	•	Example personas:
	1.	Chicago family prioritizing schools.
	2.	Remote worker wanting walkability and nightlife.
	3.	Downsizer wanting lake proximity and low maintenance.

⸻

I. Final Output Package
Return:
	•	(1) JSON quiz spec (questions, weights, areas, scoring rules)
	•	(2) Markdown summary for website and follow-up emails
	•	(3) Optional CSV of area attributes for easy tuning

⸻

Include this CTA in every result:

“Let’s dial this in — tell me your must-haves and I’ll send a hand-picked list of homes in your top area plus two smart alternates. Schedule your 15-minute Relocation Fit Call with Lucas Murphy (eXp Realty).”

⸻

End of Prompt
